## About Pouch Villa

Pouch Villa is a brand focused on offering stylish, quality, and practical pouches for everyday use.

We believe a good pouch should do more than look attractive. It should be durable, useful, easy to carry, and reliable enough for everyday needs. That is why we focus on products that combine practical design with quality customers can trust.

Our goal is to make well-made pouches accessible at affordable prices without compromising on style or usefulness. Whether for personal use, work, travel, gifting, or everyday organization, we aim to provide options that are both functional and attractive.

Customer satisfaction is also an important part of how we operate. We are committed to providing clear product information, dependable service, and a straightforward buying experience.

### Our Mission

To make quality and stylish pouches accessible to everyone while providing excellent customer service.

## Return & Warranty Policy

At Pouch Villa, we want customers to understand clearly what applies when a product is faulty, when a warranty claim is valid, and when a refund or replacement is not available.

Please read the policy below before completing a purchase.

### Returns

Faulty products must be reported and returned within **7 days of purchase**.

This return provision applies specifically to products that are found to be faulty. Any faulty item must be reported and returned within the stated 7-day period.

### Change of Mind

Pouch Villa does **not** offer a refund or replacement where a customer simply changes their mind after making a purchase.

Customers are therefore encouraged to confirm their choice before completing a purchase.

### Warranty

Pouches come with a **3-day warranty** covering **manufacturing defects only**.

The warranty applies only where the issue is the result of a manufacturing defect in the product.

### Warranty Exclusions

The warranty does **not** cover damage caused by:

- misuse;
- accidents;
- water;
- stains;
- tears;
- alterations; or
- normal wear and tear.

Damage resulting from any of the above is outside the warranty coverage.

### Resolution of Approved Warranty Claims

Where a warranty claim is approved, Pouch Villa will resolve the claim through **replacement of the affected product**.

Approved warranty claims are **not settled by cash refund**.
