# Assumptions, unknowns and client confirmations

## Confirmed inputs used

- Pouch Villa is a Calabar-based retailer specialising in phone cases and mobile accessories.
- The required experience is compatibility-first and supports saves, pickup reservations, WhatsApp preparation, store discovery and returning visitors.
- The supplied storefront photograph confirms a red and white visual identity and a physical retail presence.
- The selected design direction is the second premium mobile concept, revised with Pouch Villa branding.
- Online payment, public customer accounts, real sending and live stock quantities are out of scope.
- The Bespoke Technologies logo supplied by the user may appear in the delivery attribution/footer.

## Demonstration assumptions

- All 30 product names, product descriptions, images, prices, availability states, variants and analytics are fictional demonstration data.
- The six brands and 26 models are a practical demo catalogue, not a claim that Pouch Villa currently supports or stocks every listed device.
- Sample customers, reservations, enquiries and case requests are fictional and labelled as demonstration records.
- Reservation pickup availability is a request workflow, not a guarantee of stock or pickup readiness.
- The red interface palette is inferred from the supplied storefront branding; final brand colour values need approval.

## Explicitly rejected source claims

The available “Pouch Villa Calabar Website Offer Research Package” described Pouch Villa as a hospitality/accommodation business. That conflicts with the current client brief and supplied storefront/product evidence. No hotel, room, booking, amenity or hospitality claim from that package is used in this system.

The supplied product photograph contains third-party-styled ornamentation. It was treated only as a visual context reference and is not shipped as catalogue photography. All prototype product imagery is original fictional demonstration imagery.

## Client details still required

- Clean official Pouch Villa logo files and brand guidelines
- Exact store address, map pin and approved directions
- Opening hours and holiday hours
- Official phone and WhatsApp number
- Approved email address and social profiles
- Real products, variant names, product photography, prices and availability ownership
- Confirmed device compatibility matrix
- Reservation hold duration, pickup process and cancellation rules
- Returns, exchanges, warranty, privacy and terms wording
- Consent language and data retention period
- Staff roster, role assignments and account recovery owner
- Production domain, analytics provider and hosting/data-residency decision

Until those items are supplied, the interface shows awaiting-confirmation states or the required demonstration-data notice and never substitutes invented contact details.
